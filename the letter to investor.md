# To Investor

Dear Investor,

If you are a visionary investor looking for an ambitious, unconventional opportunity, Happy and Comet Valley may be right for you.

To discover what Happy and Comet Valley are, please visit cometvalley.org, where you can read our founders’ letter and view the presentation we created to bring our ideas and vision to life.

After reviewing those materials, we hope you will understand both our ideas and our ambition. Here, we want to emphasize one thing:

Happy is only one of the masterpieces born from our renegade spirit. For many investors, Happy alone would already represent a remarkable opportunity. But Comet Valley carries the greater vision and mission—a new kind of Google or Apple that will lead the coming age of education innovation and give our ambition a far greater stage.

And as founders, we know one thing clearly:

To realize this vision, Comet Valley needs a new Masayoshi Son or Peter Thiel—someone willing to choose visionaries as partners.

However, we are not going to approach Masayoshi Son or Peter Thiel. We believe the most wonderful choice is this:

Instead of standing on the shoulders of legends, we are inviting someone who may be unknown today, just like us, but who is equally visionary, bold, and ambitious—to create a new legend with us. 

We truly believe that money alone cannot create miracles. Visionaries can.

Let us always remember:

> The greatest power comes not from appearance, wealth, or fame, but from a visionary spirit—the ability to see an unseen future and the courage to bring it to life.

If you are the visionary we have been searching for, we invite you to contact us. We would be glad to share all the details with you.

**The Founders of Happy and Comet Valley**
