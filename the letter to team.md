# An Invitation to Build Happy With Us

Dear [Name],

We would like to invite you to consider becoming one of the earliest members of Happy’s team. Before reading further, please first view the presentation we created for investors. It explains Happy and Comet Valley, the story and innovation behind them, our strategy for building the company, and the opportunity ahead.

We believe Happy has the potential to surpass Duolingo and become the most popular language-learning brand around the world. Its success can open the door to the much larger vision of Comet Valley: bringing real innovation to education. And we also believe:

A few years from now, all of us will be able to look back and know that we helped create something once thought impossible—something valuable, respected, and much larger than any one of us.

## Why You?

Because we are friends, and our friendship is built on honesty, trust, and mutual respect. We believe that friendship can become the foundation for doing something great together.

Consider these well-known partnerships between friends:

**Steve Jobs and Steve Wozniak** combined Jobs’s product vision and entrepreneurial drive with Wozniak’s engineering talent. As friends with complementary strengths, they turned an early personal computer into Apple.

**Bill Gates and Paul Allen** were school friends united by their enthusiasm for computers. They founded Microsoft together in 1975 and helped bring software into the emerging personal-computer industry.

**Larry Page and Sergey Brin** met as graduate students at Stanford. They developed a new approach to organizing online information, built Google together, and transformed how people find knowledge.

**Mark Zuckerberg and Dustin Moskovitz** were Harvard roommates and early Facebook co-founders. Moskovitz helped expand the young platform beyond Harvard and build the organization supporting its rapid growth.

These partnerships were not identical, and success was never guaranteed. What they share is a simple beginning: friends with complementary strengths chose to pursue an ambitious idea and build it together. We believe that trust between friends can become the foundation for extraordinary work.

Like most people, we did not begin with famous names, extraordinary resources, or an easy road. But when ordinary people choose a big dream and begin working seriously toward it, they step onto a different path from their past. Our earliest team members will not simply join an established company. Together with the founders, they will help shape its product, launch, culture, organization, and future—and the experience may reshape who we become as well.


## You Do Not Need to Risk Your Current Life

We have a big ambition, but we do not want our friends to carry the financial risk of pursuing it. Joining this early group does not mean giving up your current income, investing your savings, or becoming trapped by a decision.

1. **Before investment arrives, no one is required to work for Happy.** You can continue your present job, studies, responsibilities, and daily life just as before. You may learn about the project and join our conversations, but there is no unpaid-work expectation.
2. **No team member is required to invest personal money.** You are being invited for your character, ability, and contribution—not for your capital. Happy will not ask you to pay for your position or finance the company from your savings.
3. **You are always free to leave.** If you later decide that Happy is no longer the right path for you, you may step away, and we will respect your decision. The normal terms concerning notice, confidentiality, intellectual property, salary, and equity will be explained clearly in advance and recorded in any agreements we sign together.

We want every person to join freely, after understanding both the opportunity and the uncertainty. A strong team should be built through trust and a common purpose, never through financial pressure or fear of leaving.

## Salary Plan

Our earliest team members will be among the people who helped Happy begin. As the company grows, we intend to recognize their early commitment and contributions through thoughtful compensation and equity decisions.

Our planned starting gross salary for each full-time early team member is **$3,000 per month**, before applicable taxes and deductions. It will begin only after sufficient investment has been secured and a written employment agreement has been signed. The initial salary will be funded by the capital Happy raises from investors.

As Happy builds sustainable market revenue, we will review compensation based on the company’s financial position, each person’s responsibilities and contributions, and applicable employment requirements. Greater revenue should allow us to provide stronger compensation, but increases will not be automatic.

Our two-year goal is for each established full-time team member to earn an annual salary of **$200,000**, provided that Happy’s revenue and financial position can responsibly sustain it. This is an ambitious target tied to the company’s success, not a guaranteed amount or date.

We want to begin carefully, use investors’ money responsibly, build strong revenue, and improve compensation substantially as the company becomes able to sustain it.

## Equity Plan

The same principle applies to ownership. We want the earliest people who help build Happy to have the opportunity to become long-term owners, not only employees. Every qualifying early team member is intended to receive a meaningful equity grant.

Our aim is that, if Comet Valley grows toward a future Nasdaq IPO or another major success, the people who helped create that success will share in it.

The size and terms of each grant will reflect the person’s role, commitment, joining time, and contribution. Equity will be governed by the company’s formal equity plan and individual agreements, and it will normally vest over time. Exact percentages, vesting and exercise terms, dilution, tax treatment, and the relationship between Happy and Comet Valley equity will be finalized with legal and financial guidance.

Equity offers a real opportunity, but it cannot guarantee a financial return, a particular company valuation, or an IPO. We intend to design the plan fairly, explain it clearly, and respect the contributions of the people who build with us from the beginning.


## The Path Ahead

This invitation is not asking you to decide immediately. First, we want you to understand our vision, see what we have built, ask difficult questions, and decide whether this is a path you truly want to take with us.

If you choose this extraordinary path, we will dream boldly and work seriously together. We will learn, take responsibility, overcome setbacks, and create the future together.

Perhaps a few years from now, people will call what we created a miracle. We will know it began more simply: with a small group of friends who believed, committed themselves, and started working.

We hope you will consider becoming one of them.

With trust and ambition,

**The founders of Happy and Comet Valley**
